Where we came from

Out of the dark inside

4th place in Ragnarok Origin

The name carries the whole story. We started in the dark inside, the late nights, the lost runs, the players nobody counted on. We kept showing up for each other until the dark turned bright.

Under MiniDemon we pushed the climb all the way to 4th place in Ragnarok Origin. The ranking proved the grind was real. The room we built along the way is what we actually kept.

Climb led by MiniDemon · also known as MD3mon, Panthera

What this place is

A hangout, first. A guild, second.

Once the ranking was set, the grind got quieter and the room stayed loud. People drop in after a raid, type whatever is on their mind, toss a reaction, lose a dice roll, and stay way longer than they meant to. That is the whole point.
