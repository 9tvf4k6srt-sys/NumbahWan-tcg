What we are

A paradox, on purpose.

Most guilds pick a lane. Sweaty, or social. We refused. You get the tryhard clear speed of a top-4 roster and a room that roasts each other at 2am over nothing. Show up to compete, you will be pushed. Show up to belong, the bench by the fire already has your name on it. Both. Always both.

  • RuthlessWe hit 4th because nobody phoned it in. Standards stay high and they are not negotiable.
  • FamilyNo carry culture, no rank-shaming. We win as a crew or we lose loud together.
  • OpenVeteran or first week, English or Thai or Japanese. If you bring up, you fit in.
