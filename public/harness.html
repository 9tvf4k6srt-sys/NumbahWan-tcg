    How the loop works

    • commit/merge → observers snapshot telemetry, efficiency, trends
    • merge-diff → IMPROVED / STEADY / REGRESSED vs the previous merge
    • improve-proposer → a trend firing 3+ runs earns one doctrine-edit proposal
    • human → approves; the doctrine (and the next agent) gets smarter
    • golden eval → evals/ recomputes the token savings and re-checks doctrine against reality (6 checks)
