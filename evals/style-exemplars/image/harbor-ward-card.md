# Golden image prompt — Harbor Ward card art

A Taipei night-market card stall at blue hour, shot on a 35mm lens at f/1.8
with shallow depth of field. Steam from a noodle cart catches the stall's
bare tungsten bulb; a single trading card lies face-up on the counter glass,
its foil edge picking up the red of a temple-roof lantern across the lane.
MRT signage glows out of focus in the background, mountain haze above the
rooflines. Muted green-and-gold palette, true black shadows, no post glow.
