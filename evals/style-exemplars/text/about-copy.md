# The NumbahWan market

Card prices come from the auction house ledger, not from us. Every sale since
launch is public: bidder, price, timestamp. When the Harbor Ward set dropped on
June 12, rare pulls opened at 400 credits and settled near 250 within two weeks
because 500 copies exist and every one was accounted for on-chain.

Supply stays fixed by design. A boss-dungeon rare is minted once per kill, and
kill counts are public. That is why the ledger matters more than the hype: you
can check scarcity yourself before you bid.

New sets land every six weeks. Patch notes list exact drop rates. If a rate
changes, the note says what it was and what it became.
